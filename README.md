# Privacy Policy for Tasbih

**Effective Date:** November 26, 2026  
**App Name:** Tasbih  
**Package ID:** `app.tasbih.dhikr`

---

## 1. Overview

**Tasbih** is an offline, minimalist Islamic Tasbih and Dhikr counter designed for peaceful contemplation and daily remembrance. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping your experience completely private.

**We do not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personal data.**

---

## 2. Information Collection and Use

Tasbih operates **100% offline** on your device:

- **No Personal Information:** The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, account login, or location.
- **No Analytics or Tracking:** The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, or crash-telemetry services.
- **No Network Transmission:** Your dhikr counts and preferences stay strictly on your device and are never uploaded to external servers.

---

## 3. Local Device Storage

Tasbih stores only lightweight app settings locally on your device using Android's standard application storage (`AsyncStorage`), including:

- Reminder status and scheduled reminder times
- Display/theme preferences

You can remove all locally stored preferences at any time by clearing the app's data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

---

## 4. Permissions We Request

Tasbih only requests permissions strictly required for core functionality:

- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used solely to display local, on-device daily Dhikr reminders scheduled on your device. No remote push servers are used.
- **Vibration (`VIBRATE`):** Used for subtle haptic feedback while counting beads and for reminder notifications.
- **Run at Startup (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`):** Used only so Android can restore your locally scheduled daily Tasbih reminder alarms after your phone restarts.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

Tasbih does not display ads and does not share any data with third-party data brokers, advertisers, or analytics providers. Distribution occurs solely through Google Play Store, which is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Because Tasbih collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated in the future, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised Effective Date.

---

## 8. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the Tasbih app, please reach out through the developer contact options on the **Tasbih** Google Play Store listing.
